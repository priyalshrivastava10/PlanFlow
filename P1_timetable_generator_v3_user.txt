Create a day-wise study timetable.

Subjects and difficulty: {subjects_with_difficulty}
Exam dates: {exam_dates}
Start date: {start_date}
Daily study hours: {daily_hours}
Preferred study time: {preferred_time}
Breaks: {break_rule}

Rules:
1. Total study time per day must not exceed {daily_hours} hours (breaks are not study time).
2. Hard subjects get more hours than Medium, Medium more than Easy.
3. Focus on the subject with the nearest exam first.
4. Add breaks as separate rows, as per {break_rule}.
5. The day before each exam must be a revision day for that subject.
6. Do not schedule a subject after its exam date.
7. If two exams are on the same date, split time between them.

Output format: ONLY a Markdown table, no extra text, with columns:
| Date | Time | Subject | Task | Duration |
After the table, add one line: "Total study hours per day: <list>".

Example 1
Input: Maths (Hard), Physics (Easy); exams Maths 25 Nov, Physics 27 Nov; start 23 Nov; 2 hours/day; 6 PM to 9 PM; break 10 min after every hour
Output:
| Date | Time | Subject | Task | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 23 Nov | 6:00-7:00 PM | Maths | Learn core formulas | 1 hr |
| 23 Nov | 7:00-7:10 PM | Break | Rest | 10 min |
| 23 Nov | 7:10-8:10 PM | Maths | Solve numericals | 1 hr |
| 24 Nov | 6:00-7:00 PM | Maths | Revision (exam tomorrow) | 1 hr |
| 24 Nov | 7:00-7:10 PM | Break | Rest | 10 min |
| 24 Nov | 7:10-8:10 PM | Physics | Read concepts | 1 hr |
Total study hours per day: 23 Nov: 2, 24 Nov: 2
